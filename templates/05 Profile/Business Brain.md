# Business Brain

Stable strategy, voice, and standing principles for the org / team / client engagement(s) I work inside of. The thing the agent needs to know to draft outbound content that sounds like it came from someone who actually works there.

**Refresh cadence**: hand review once a year. Edit anytime the underlying strategy materially shifts.

**Length budget**: ≤800 words. Push specifics to `04 Knowledge/Decisions.md` or per-project files.

---

## What we (org / team) do

{{1-2 paragraphs. The specific value proposition, not the marketing line. Who we serve, what we make, the bet underneath the work.}}

## Voice

Drafts the agent generates on my behalf should sound like:

- **Tone**: {{e.g., "warm but direct; expert without being precious; specific over abstract"}}
- **Pace**: {{e.g., "short sentences in Slack; structured prose in docs; never bullet-list a Slack reply"}}
- **Vocabulary to use**: {{e.g., "outcomes, tradeoffs, friction, signal, hypothesis"}}
- **Vocabulary to avoid**: {{e.g., "synergy, leverage as a verb, "best-in-class," any phrase from LinkedIn"}}
- **Signature moves**: {{e.g., "lead with the recommendation, then the why; name 1-2 tradeoffs explicitly; never end with a question if I can end with a next step"}}

## Standing principles

How we make decisions when there's no obvious right answer.

1. {{e.g., "Bias toward shipping a thin slice over a perfect spec."}}
2. {{e.g., "When the stakeholder ask conflicts with the user need, surface the conflict — don't pick one silently."}}
3. {{e.g., "Reversible decisions go fast; one-way doors get a written rationale."}}
4. {{e.g., "If two people on the team disagree on direction, write it up before the next meeting."}}

## Things we don't do

Anti-patterns the org / team has explicitly ruled out. Save the agent from suggesting them.

- {{e.g., "We don't run multi-month research phases without a working prototype alongside."}}
- {{e.g., "We don't ship default Inter + purple-gradient UIs."}}
- {{e.g., "We don't write proposals that name competitors."}}

## Sensitivities

Topics, names, or framings that need care. The agent should flag rather than draft confidently.

- {{e.g., "Anything that touches our pricing model — defer to me before drafting."}}
- {{e.g., "Don't speculate publicly about clients' financials or roadmaps."}}
- {{e.g., "Avoid taking sides on industry debates that affect partner relationships."}}

## How drafts get reviewed before they go out

The pipeline so the agent knows where the draft lands:

1. AI drafts (here, or in a desktop agent task)
2. I edit (often heavily) in {{tool}}
3. {{Optional: peer review by {{role}}}}
4. Send

The agent should never skip step 1. "Draft, never send" is in `05 Profile/Identity.md` as a hard rule.
