# Think OS Home

Welcome back. This is the front page for your personal context OS: a place to see what matters now, find the right note quickly, and keep your agent grounded in how you work.

Start here when you are getting oriented. Jump deeper when you already know what you need.

## Vault Map

| Area | What goes here | When to open it |
|---|---|---|
| `01 Now/` | Current focus, task inbox, work log | Starting the day or weekly review |
| `02 Projects/` | Project index and one note per project | Starting, resuming, or switching work |
| `03 People/` | Lightweight personal CRM | Before meetings, drafts, or stakeholder questions |
| `04 Knowledge/` | Decisions and reusable learnings | Before recommending an approach |
| `05 Profile/` | Identity, voice, business context | Updating personal context or voice |
| `90 System/` | Agent instructions and connector map | Setup, debugging, or changing rules |
| `99 Archive/` | Rotated logs and dormant notes | Looking backward |

## Gentle Upkeep

- Daily: glance at [[01 Now/Current Focus|Current Focus]] and [[01 Now/Tasks|Tasks]]
- Weekly: roll [[01 Now/Current Focus|Current Focus]] forward and scan [[02 Projects/Project Index|Project Index]]
- Quarterly: archive [[01 Now/Work Log|Work Log]], prune stale [[90 System/Connectors|Connectors]], and promote stable [[05 Profile/Voice Profile|Voice Profile]] patterns
