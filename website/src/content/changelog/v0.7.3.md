### Highlights

- `ConversationStrip` and `VaultTree` no longer present a fabricated bio as the maintainer's real life
- Demo content now uses Sam Reyes — Engineering Lead at Cardinal Studio, Brooklyn — explicitly fictional
- The references that remain on the site are the ones that are actually true

### What changed

While grounding the demo in real names, the implementation agent invented a persona for the maintainer — a job title, a city, a background, and current work items ("Q3 internal AI roadmap to Maya by Friday") that were simply not true. For a project whose whole pitch is honest personal context, that's the worst possible demo content.

The fix is a clearly fictional persona and a tightened disclaimer under the conversation strip: "Sample vault content. Sam Reyes and Cardinal Studio are fictional — your real vault has your real notes." The legitimate references stay — "Designed and built by Dhiraj Sapkal" in the footer, "Built at Think Company" in the trust bar — because those are actually true.

**Upgrade:** website-only release; nothing to do.
